# Prospective fixed-budget audit of archived verifiers on fresh dossiers

## Claim and design

This is a local, synthetic, finite-corpus experiment. The old P4 checker and profile bytes are the instruments. All case identities, evidence data, claim aliases and simulated collection contexts are new. Two contexts use each of the supported W1 and W2 profiles. The four contexts exercise new dossiers within two supported workflow profiles. They are not four new verifier profiles or independently deployed workflows. There is one dossier per unit. Twelve probe strata have four cases per context. The design intentionally exercises representation risks, including controls in flagged strata and without flags. Stratum frequencies are fixed by design. They do not estimate prevalence in field data.

The primary outcome is O15, the claim-source obligation. For a comparable pair with identical O15 judgement, an agreement error is the shared judgement differing from the independent, frozen O15 reference. `U` can be a determinate expected checker judgement under a specified sufficient-evidence rule. An absent or defective reference receipt is a study integrity failure, with no clean certification. The full 16-entry vector and aggregate are retained as scope checks. Discrepancies outside O15 are reported and can invalidate clean-context interpretation. This is a different experiment from P4 and prospective to the new checker outcomes. No historical case is rerun and no original benchmark score is recomputed.

The claim-source representation contract sanctions equivalent direct and one-level alias forms. It resolves literal full and suffix keys symmetrically and rejects conflicts. Where historical implementation assumptions differ, a shared error is a result relative to this explicit experimental contract. It is not a claim that the historically under-specified interface dictated the experimental answer.

## Review policies

Each policy gets at most 28 case adjudications in each context of 48, for a ceiling of 112 across the four contexts. Each case costs one adjudication by design. Every policy starts with all comparable O15 disagreements as certainty cases, if these fit. The pure disagreement policy stops after this panel and reports unused slots. A policy cannot certify a completed audit when a required panel exceeds the budget or a pair is incomplete. For oversubscribed panels, the frozen within-context order determines which cases can be inspected and the shortfall is explicit.

- **Disagreement only.** Review the disagreement panel, with no residual draw.
- **Disagreement plus uniform sampling.** Review the disagreement panel, then fill the available slots from all comparable O15 agreements in the frozen uniform random order.
- **Disagreement plus representation screening and uniform sampling.** Review the disagreement panel, then all agreements flagged on raw claim evidence. Fill the remaining slots from unflagged comparable agreements using the same order restricted to that frame.

Freeze a context-specific uniform permutation using `secrets.SystemRandom`, drawn once before candidate execution and kept as a row-order receipt. Its restrictions to alternative residual frames provide common random numbers. The frame definitions depend on the input evidence and the checker outputs, independently of the draw. The policy does not consult strata, oracle labels or per-case diagnostics. The representation rule flags a used `alias:` token when the suffix exists and the literal token does not, when both exist and resolve to different raw targets, or when a mapped target is itself an alias token. Cases with missing screening inputs or invalid screening shapes carry an explicit screen status. A flagged case may conform to the reference.

## Analysis

1. Commit the frame, actual input dossier manifest, checker and profile bindings, this plan, the screen and selection code, the reference receipt and source, and the uniform permutations before running the candidate pair. Keep the receipt out of the selection process. Commit selection lists and binds before opening the reference for scoring. A rerun requires a separately identified execution and preservation of the original outputs.
2. The primary comparison is the number of agreement errors found by the two budget-filling policies at 28 slots per context, with signed difference. Report errors missed, flagged reviews without an agreement error, denominator, agreement coverage, full-vector/aggregate deviations and the result for the disagreement-only policy. Report per-context and pooled finite counts. The full adjudication was sealed to calculate the evaluation denominator, while the simulated budget limits what each review policy could have observed. No review-time savings or field error rates are estimated.
3. For each completed context and budget-filling policy, give the deterministic interval for the total number of O15 agreement errors, from observed agreement errors to observed errors plus all unreviewed comparable agreements. The residual draw is a one-look simple random sample. Compute the exact hypergeometric one-sided upper bound for the residual count using level 0.0125 per context, and add confirmed certainty errors. For the primary screening policy, summing the four bounds gives a simultaneous family-wise upper bound using Bonferroni at 0.05. Uniform-only bounds are descriptive comparisons under their own policy. The pure disagreement policy reports the deterministic interval. Missing candidate pairs block certificates. No post hoc changes to draw size, comparisons or thresholds are allowed in this experiment.
4. The illustrative finite-frame tolerance is no more than six O15 agreement errors per context (12.5% of its 48 scheduled dossiers) chosen as a stress-design threshold rather than a deployment threshold. `VIOLATED` requires observed lower count greater than six. `MET` requires a valid upper at most six. Otherwise report `NOT_ESTABLISHED`. The scientific conclusion is the measured policy yield, residual uncertainty and any counterexamples, even if the tolerance cannot be shown or the screened policy underperforms.

Procedure identifies independence of code-author roles and outcomes. The case generator and reference sources are written separately from the runner. All use a shared experiment specification. There is no independently commissioned adjudication, no human review-time measurement and no claim of external validity from this synthetic dataset. The archived P4 and P5 qualification and documentary anchors retain their original interpretation.
